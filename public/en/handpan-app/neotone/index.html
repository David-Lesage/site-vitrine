How many scales do you really play?

When the notes move, don’t you get lost?

I did. On a Neotone, you change scale and the path is gone: your hand hunts around, your head spins. That’s cognitive overload. So you go back to the scales you know, and an instrument that could play anything ends up playing only a small part of what it can.

Questions

What people ask me.

Which Neotones?

The Neotone¹ and the Neotone Mutant. And any MIDI instrument.

Do I need Studio to connect my Neotone?

No. Connecting it by USB or MIDI is free, with your playing shown in real time and the exercises for learning your scales. Studio opens up Neotone Connection, Logic mode and the duos.

Which devices?

On a computer or tablet, in your browser. On a phone, the screen is too small to see your handpan and its chords properly. On iPhone and iPad, Apple stops browsers from receiving an instrument plugged in by USB: to connect a digital handpan, open the app in Web MIDI Browser, free on the App Store.

I also have an acoustic handpan.

The app sees them together: that’s the hybrid duo, in Studio. And your acoustic gets everything that’s free in the app.

What if I cancel?

We take your card details when you sign up, but you’re not charged before the free month ends. After that, you can cancel whenever you like, with effect from the following month.

I’ve never studied music theory.

You start with the colours and the emotions. The theory comes when you ask for it.
