Singing while you play the handpan means listening to two things at once: the instrument and your own voice. And we hear our own voice poorly. The **Forbrain headset** is made for that: hearing yourself live, more clearly, so you can work on it.

This applies to **every handpan**, acoustic or electronic: the headset doesn't plug into the instrument, it listens to your voice.

## What it is

![Black and blue Forbrain headset: a band goes behind the neck, two pads sit in front of the ears, a boom mic comes out of the left unit](/images/prod-forbrain-1.webp)

*The Forbrain: notice the two pads that sit **in front of** the ears, not inside them, and the boom mic near the mouth.*

It's a **<abbr title="sound travels through the bones of the face instead of through the air to the eardrum">bone-conduction</abbr>** headset with a microphone. You speak or sing, and your voice comes straight back to you through the bones in front of your ears.

On the way, your voice goes through a **dynamic filter** that lifts the highs and softens the lows. You hear yourself differently, more clearly.

It's **standalone**: no screen, no app. Put it on, switch it on, sing.

## What it's for (and what it isn't)

It's **not a stage mic**. It's a **training tool**.

How to use it: a few minutes a day, reading aloud or singing, to work on your **voice**, **diction**, **pitch** and **focus**.

With the handpan, it's a good companion for sessions where you set your voice over a scale. If you're first looking for the right key to sing in, also read [transposing to sing with your handpan](/en/blog/transposer-pour-chanter-handpan/).

I don't expect anything medical from it: I recommend it for voice work, nothing more.

## What about the Neotone headphones?

Two different uses. Headphones plugged into an electronic handpan let you hear **the instrument**: see [playing and singing with headphones on the Neotone](/en/blog/jouer-et-chanter-au-casque-neotone/) and [which headphones for the Neotone](/en/blog/quel-casque-choisir-neotone/). The Forbrain lets you hear **your voice**.
