**This article is about the calabash**, the percussion instrument at the heart of my Now Groove rhythm method. No handpan here: this is the cover I designed to carry a calabash.

I have played the calabash as a percussion instrument since 2012. I take it everywhere: lessons, workshops, the stage, set on its mat in front of my handpans. The Now Groove cover is the object I designed so it can travel with me.

## Where the Now Groove calabash comes from

On the calabash, I was missing the snare and the <abbr title="A pair of cymbals worked with the foot, which keeps the beat in a drum kit.">hi-hat</abbr>. I solved it with **plastic eggs**: a hack that became a way of playing, then a way of teaching.

It became **[Now Groove](/en/cours/)** in 2021: a rhythm method built on call-and-response and visual emoticons. The playing kit has three parts: the calabash, its **mat**, and its egg shakers.

![The Now Groove calabash kit: a half-sphere calabash engraved with the Now Groove logo, upside down on a wide grey mat, with two blue egg-shaped shakers](/images/prod-calebasse.jpeg)

*The playing kit: the calabash upside down on its mat, and the two eggs that play snare and hi-hat. Note the Now Groove logo engraved on the calabash.*

## A rare calabash, a cover made for it

The Now Groove kit calabashes are **rare**: a calabash 46 cm in diameter or more, with a thick 1.5 to 2 cm rim, is very hard to find, especially in Europe. They come from West Africa (Niger, Burkina Faso, Mali). Each one is reworked by hand by Kamou, at Djoliba Percussion in Toulouse, who refines the rim so it fits its mat.

The cover carries that whole set in one bag: the calabash, the mat, the eggs. I **conceived and designed it entirely**, with the support of my partners at Djoliba in Toulouse, especially Yu-Tin.

## What the cover does

The cover is a **backpack** I conceived and designed myself. It brings together:

- **Built-in mat function**: the playing mat is part of the cover.
- **Detachable straps**: carry it on your back, or take the straps off.
- **Storage pocket**, closed with a hook-and-loop strip, for the eggs, accessories and the paper method.
- **Velvet lining** on the mat side.
