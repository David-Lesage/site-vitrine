**This article is about the acoustic handpan**: it covers miking an instrument that has no jack output. An electronic handpan such as the [Neotone](/en/neotone/) plugs in directly, no microphone needed.

Amplifying an acoustic handpan is a real challenge. General-purpose mics capture the **overtones** poorly, and **feedback** creeps in as soon as you turn up the volume.

The Muling set is designed for this specific instrument. I tested it in depth, and that's what convinced me to represent it. Here is how it works and how to set it up.

## Why there's no feedback

Feedback is the howl you get when a mic picks up the speaker's sound and loops it back.

The MP-1 pickup is **not an ambient mic**. It is a surface condenser microphone: it captures vibrations **in direct contact with the shell**.

The result: outside noise and other instruments barely get through. You can turn up the volume on stage without fearing feedback, and the sound stays faithful even in a noisy venue.

## Setup, step by step

![An acoustic handpan on the floor, with the two red Muling pickups and the connected HMP-2 preamp in front of it](/images/muling-sur-handpan.webp)
*The complete system next to the instrument: two pickups, two cables, one preamp. Nothing else.*

1. **Place the pickups on the shell.** They hold **by magnet**: on and off in one move. They are the size of a fingertip and disappear while you play. The recommended positions are drawn in the supplied manual.
2. **Plug them into the preamp.** The active pickup goes into **CH1**, the second into **CH2** with the switch set to **ON**.
3. **Balance the two volumes.** CH1 and CH2 each have their own knob.
4. **Go out with a single** 6.35 mm jack cable to your speaker, mixing desk or audio interface.

![The HMP-2 preamp from the front: large SHAPE knob, CH1 and CH2 volumes, centre switch and LED](/images/muling-preampli-face.webp)
*The preamp from the front. Note the large SHAPE knob at the top, the two volumes below and the small "9V for CH2" switch in the middle.*

**The SHAPE knob sculpts the sound.** At 0, you keep the original tone. Turning it clockwise boosts lows and highs while cutting the mids: handy for cutting through a busy mix.

## Battery or mains

- **9 V battery** (6F22 type), housed inside the case: about **20 hours of playing**, no power cable. I personally use a rechargeable battery.
- **Mains**: 9 V adapter, 300 mA minimum, 5.5 × 2.1 mm plug, **centre-negative polarity**. It is **not included**: buy it separately and double-check those figures.

## Read before plugging in

These are the manufacturer's warnings, not mere tips:

- **Never** plug a passive pickup into the **CH1** input: it delivers power at all times.
- For an H1-type passive pickup, **CH2 must be set to OFF**.
- **Reversed polarity** on the mains adapter destroys the unit permanently.
- The pickups work **only with the Muling preamp**: it's a complete system, not a pickup to add to an existing rig.
